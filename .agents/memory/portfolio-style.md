---
name: Andrew Solutions visual direction
description: Style constraints for the personal portfolio’s space-themed design.
---

Keep the portfolio simple and eye-catching: dark space with stars and friendly cartoon planets; avoid geometric orbit lines, decorative numeric labels, and visible divider bands between the hero and About section.

**Why:** Andrew asked for a less cluttered, playful planets-and-stars look.

**How to apply:** For future visual changes to Andrew Solutions, use one continuous starfield across the hero and About sections, and keep project links and screenshots prominent.
